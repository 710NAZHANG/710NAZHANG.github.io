Bonding at surfaces

Revealing the role of bonding-state occupation in surface adsorption and chemisorption.

Research focus

This work investigates how bonding states influence chemisorption on metal–nitrogen–carbon (M–N–C) surfaces.

Key findings

  • The slope of the scaling relationship between OH and O is about twice that between OH and OOH. This is mainly associated with the contributions of dxz and dyz orbitals in the M–N–C system.
  • Variations in adsorption across transition metals in the M–N–C system are mainly attributed to bonding-state occupation, rather than the antibonding-state occupation described by the d-band model.

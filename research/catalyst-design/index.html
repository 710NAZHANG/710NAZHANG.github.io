Research focus

My work on catalyst design includes single-atom alloy nanocatalysts for CO2 reduction to ethylene and bioinspired sulfur-surrounded iron catalysts for ammonia synthesis.

Single-atom alloy catalysts

In the single-atom alloy study, electron-sufficient and electron-deficient metals are combined to construct bifunctional adsorption sites for reactions involving oxygenated reactants such as CO2 and hydrocarbon products such as C2H4.

The adsorption abilities of electron-deficient and electron-sufficient elements are mainly governed by unoccupied bonding states and occupied antibonding states, respectively.

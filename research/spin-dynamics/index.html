Ultrafast spin dynamics

Research focus

This work explores the ultrafast spin dynamics of magnetic clusters, including laser-induced spin flips, spin-flip transfer and spin crossover in cobalt–benzene clusters.

Key findings

  • Spin-flip scenarios, in which the spin direction changes, are realized in the studied structures under different sets of laser pulses.
  • Spin-flip transfer and spin crossover, involving a change in spin multiplicity, are achieved in Co2Bz and CoBz+, respectively.

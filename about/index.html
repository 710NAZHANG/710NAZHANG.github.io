About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Oklahoma, United States. My research focuses on plasmonics, photoexcited hot-carrier dynamics, and light-driven catalysis, with a particular interest in understanding how light–matter interactions influence electronic excitations, interfacial charge transfer, and chemical reactions at the nanoscale.

My work primarily employs first-principles calculations and real-time time-dependent density functional theory (rt-TDDFT) to investigate ultrafast electron dynamics, plasmon-induced hot-carrier generation, and their roles in surface chemistry. I am particularly interested in plasmonic nanostructures, two-dimensional MXenes, and metal–molecule interfaces.
